How it works. Songs come from albums saved in Your Library → Albums, plus any playlist whose name starts with 💿 (CDs recreated song by song because the original isn't on Spotify — you can make your own: name a playlist “💿 Something” and reload albums). Ratings are stored as five private playlists named “My Ratings ★1” … “My Ratings ★5”. A song is “rated” if it's in any of them — so you can even rate from the Spotify app by dragging a song into one of those playlists. Playback uses Spotify Connect, so music plays on whatever device you pick and this page is just the remote.
